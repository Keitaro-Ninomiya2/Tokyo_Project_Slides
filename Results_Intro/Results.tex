\begin{frame}{Preview of results}
    \begin{itemize}[<+->]
        \item Workplaces relied more on internal transfer of staff rather than hiring externally.
        \begin{itemize}[<+->]
            \item A one-unit increase in draftees caused about twice as many internal transfers as external hires (0.41 vs.\ 0.22).
            \item Internal employees from closer sections were more likely to move.
            \item Workplaces used promotion (0.26 units) and bonuses (0.13 units) to retain staff.

        \end{itemize}

        \item Transferred workers outperformed their peers at both origin and destination units.
        \begin{itemize}[<+->]
            \item Rank gain/Postwar survival were 12.1pp/10.5pp (vs.\ destination peers) and 18.0pp/16.3pp (vs.\ donor peers) higher.
        \end{itemize}
    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
