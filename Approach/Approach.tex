\begin{frame}{Identifying who filled draft-induced vacancies}
    \begin{itemize}[<+->]
        \item We study how draft-induced vacancies in a public-sector organization with more than 40{,}000 employees (e.g., the Tokyo civil service) were filled.

        \vspace{0.5em}

         \item We compare vacancy-filling patterns across organizationally adjacent sections in response to draft-induced employee exits.
        \begin{itemize}[<+->]
            \item The drafting process was undisclosed to the public $\Rightarrow$ drafting is as good as random after conditioning on occupation and section assignment.
        \end{itemize}

        \item We compare how vacancies are filled through internal transfers versus external hiring.

    \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
